What we build

One custom system, built for the way your desk works.

We do not sell a feature list. We learn your niche and build the parts your desk needs, from first search to the reply that books a call, all in your name and inside your ATS.

01 · Source

Sourcing built for your niche

Custom search and enrichment tuned to the roles your desk actually fills, across Apollo, the open web, GitHub, and portfolios. We find the people your competitors miss and verify them before they reach you.

02 · Score

Scoring and outreach in your voice

Candidates ranked against your real brief with a written rationale, not a black-box number. Outreach drafted in your desk's tone and your clients' language, so it reads like your recruiter wrote it.

03 · Reply

Replies and follow-up, handled

Inbound replies qualified in-thread and routed to your recruiter when they are warm. Multi-touch follow-up on anyone who goes quiet, so nothing leaks out of the pipeline.
